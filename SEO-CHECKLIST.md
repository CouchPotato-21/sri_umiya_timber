## 1. Google Business Profile — THE #1 priority 🥇
This is what makes you show up in Google Maps and the "local pack" (the map + 3 businesses) for
"timber near me" / "door shop near me". Far more impactful than anything else here.

- [ ] Go to https://business.google.com and **claim/verify** the existing "SRI UMIYA TIMBER" listing.
- [ ] Set primary category **Timber merchant**; add **Door supplier**, **Wood supplier**, **Wood floor installation service**.
- [ ] Enter exact hours (Mon–Sat 9AM–7PM), phone (+91 96114 67272), and website (your new domain).
- [ ] Upload **20–30 real photos** of your work, workshop, and team. Businesses with photos get far more calls.
- [ ] Add your services (doors, frames, shutters, CNC carving, flooring, railings) with short descriptions.
- [ ] Post updates occasionally (new work, offers) — treat it like a mini social feed.

## 2. Google Search Console — so you can see your rankings
- [ ] Go to https://search.google.com/search-console, add your domain, and verify it.
      (Verification usually gives you a `<meta>` tag — send it to me and I'll add it to the site, or add via DNS.)
- [ ] Submit your sitemap: `https://www.sriumiyatimber.com/sitemap.xml`
- [ ] Check back weekly to see which searches bring visitors and which pages rank.

## 3. Get listed on Indian business directories (use the EXACT same name, address, phone everywhere)
Consistent "NAP" (Name/Address/Phone) across the web is a strong local ranking signal.
- [ ] **JustDial** — biggest one for Indian local search.
- [ ] **IndiaMART** — great for timber/building-material buyers.
- [ ] **Sulekha**
- [ ] **Bing Places** (https://www.bingplaces.com)
- [ ] Make sure the name is always exactly **Sri Umiya Timber** and the address matches the website footer.

## 4. Get Google reviews ⭐
- [ ] Ask happy customers to leave a Google review (share your GBP review link on WhatsApp).
- [ ] Aim for a steady trickle — reviews boost both ranking and trust. Reply to every one.

## 5. Backlinks (other sites linking to you)
- [ ] Ask architects, interior designers, and builders you work with to list/link your site.
- [ ] Local Bangalore business blogs or directories.
- [ ] Your Instagram bio should link the website (and vice-versa).
